Philosophy

Fewer decisions,
better ones.

Good interiors are not built from more — more colour, more pattern, more furniture. They come from restraint: the right proportions, honest materials, and light left to do most of the work.

We design every room as one continuous idea, from the joinery to the door handles, so a home reads as a single, quiet thought rather than a collection of choices.

Services

Interior Architecture

02

Interior Design

Material palettes, furniture, lighting and styling, curated as one considered scheme rather than a room-by-room patchwork.

Process

How a project unfolds.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We visit the space, listen to how you live, and set the brief and budget before any design begins.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Spatial layouts, material direction and a considered scheme, presented as one coherent idea for the whole home.

  3. 03

    Detail & specification

    Every finish, fitting and piece of furniture drawn, sourced and specified down to the last handle.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    We manage contractors and installation on site, so the home you receive matches the one we designed.
